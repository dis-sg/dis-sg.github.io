Stoney is an anthropomorphised Singapore Stone, described as “the National Museum's longest-running mystery come to life”. Housed at the NMS, the Singapore Stone is one of Singapore’s national treasures, bearing undeciphered inscriptions believed to be either Old Javanese, Sanskrit or Tamil.

<figure>
<img src="https://i.imgur.com/VTByGRi.png" alt="The Singapore Stone">
<figcaption>The Singapore Stone <a href="https://biblioasia.nlb.gov.sg/all-sections/vol-21-issue-1-apr-jun-2025-origins-badang-strongman-singapore-stone/" target="_blank">(Source)</a></figcaption>
</figure>

Stoney was introduced in Oct 2026 as part of the NMS’ 139th anniversary celebration, which included the reopening of the revamped Singapore History Gallery. 

Where Stoney differs from the Singapore Stone in design is that the mascot only has inscriptions on its right side of its body. Stoney is one of the few Singapore mascots to have a default troubled or worried expression.

Stoney enjoyed extensive publicity during his launch campaign, including being used as the profile photo for NMS’ social media pages. A video series showed Stoney trying to uncover his past by visiting places such as Little Gulin, only to find that the granite there is not the same material as its sandstone body.
